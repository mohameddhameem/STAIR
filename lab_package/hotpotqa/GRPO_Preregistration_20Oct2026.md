# GRPO Pre-Registration — Stage-Level Routing
**Dated 20 October 2026 · amends the 13 October plan · drafted for Jonathan's review — not registered until he approves**

## 1. Objective and method
Train the two routers (Ret, QA1 — one LoRA adapter each, deployed anatomy identical to SFT) with GRPO: decisions are **sampled** from the router's current probabilities during training, the chosen path executes, and one end-of-episode reward per rollout scores the **delivered answer**. Group advantage = (R′ − group mean) / group std; one batched update covers both routers. No labels anywhere; gold answers appear only inside the reward. Gold passages never enter the training signal (diagnostics only). No QA2 adapter (see §7).

## 2. Data
Our frozen split of the 7,405-question HotpotQA file: **train 4,814 (65%) / validation 1,110 (15%) / test 1,481 (20%)**, seed 20260729 — the same files as every SFT result, so the GRPO-vs-SFT comparison is on identical data. The test split remains held out (read only at declared readouts).

## 3. Reward (unchanged from the certified SFT (label) + Reward recipe)
R′ = tokenF1(delivered, gold) − 0.10 · C_path / 929.
- λ = 0.10 (registered 11 Oct). C_SS = 929 = always-strong policy cost (814 + 115, no weak-first component).
- Path costs are the certified `PATH` dict (imported, never retyped): WW 80 · WS 195 · SW 894 · **SS 929 = C_SS itself** (the certified reward charges the double-escalation path at the always-strong price). The budget term max(0, C/C_SS − 1) (B replaced by C_SS as a definition, 21 Oct) is therefore **literally zero on every path** — "hinge silent at K=1" holds exactly.
- The risk term remains dormant. tokenF1 is computed by the certified `lenient_score.py` (imported, never reimplemented); multiple golds take the max.

## 4. Execution: replay of the certified execution record
Training and validation consume the **certified Config B execution record** (`runs/config_b` stores — minted by `run_labels_agenttts.py` on the SMU cluster with the Config B executors: Ret Qwen2.5-7B/72B · QA Llama-3.2-3B/3.1-70B): every task-model generation for all four paths, produced under greedy decoding. Strong outcomes are replayed, never called. Replay returns the record by construction; GRPO and every SFT result consume the **same record**, which is the comparability guarantee.

**Smoke-test record (20 Oct, with correction):** (a) **Determinism confirmed** — two independent live runs of the grid pipeline (`run_labels4.py`, Config A executors, 10 train instances) matched **60/60 byte-identical**: greedy generation is exactly repeatable within one software environment. (b) **Correction logged:** the first comparison that evening (certified-vs-live, 20/60) inadvertently compared the Config B record against a Config A live rerun — different executors — and is void as replay evidence; no drift claim is made. (c) **Pending, non-blocking:** a proper Config B live-equivalence check = a ~10-instance slice rerun of `run_labels_agenttts.py` on the cluster (its smoke machinery exists), compared against the record. Audit trail: `runs/smoke_grpo_replay`, `runs/smoke_grpo_replay2`.

## 5. Success bar and kill criterion
- **Primary verdict (unchanged metric):** validation composed **lenient-match accuracy at cost**, compared against the certified SFT benchmark **76.85 @ 70.8% of always-SS cost** (same split, same ruler, thresholds locked by the same rule).
- **Kill criterion (training health; currency = delivered-path val mean-R′, greedy decisions at both stages):** GRPO must beat the both-reward pair's **0.6892** (computed 21 Oct from the pair's stored val_scores, greedy at both stages, delivered-path R′ — feasibility report v1.1) within **3 epochs** or training stops and Plan C is considered.
- **Measured scale (feasibility v1.1, val):** always-SS policy 0.6569 → both-reward pair (greedy) 0.6892 → oracle (best path per question) 0.7790. Oracle context: at its best-R′ choices the oracle delivers 81.0% lenient accuracy at mean cost 240 — the measured headroom of routing on this dataset.
- *Recorded to prevent confusion:* the reward-training manifests' `best_val_meanR` values (e.g. 0.7301) are **stage-local expected R′ under assumption A2** — a different quantity, never compared against GRPO's number.

## 8. Thresholds (after training; where θ is born)
STORE → SWEEP → LOCK on validation, no sampling from STORE onward.
- **Rule B (the selection rule, same as the SFT rule):** highest composed accuracy first; candidates within one standard error of the best count as tied; among ties, the cheapest θ wins. Lock before test.
- **Rule A ("cheapest θ clearing the always-strong bar") runs as a free sensitivity analysis** on the same sweep curve; its θ and test result are reported alongside. The rule is never re-picked after seeing test results.

## 9. Training configuration
- **Warm start (primary):** from the label-trained adapters; one additional **from-scratch run with guards** (entropy floor, LR 1e-5, drift check, checkpoint by objective) to test whether exploration alone can lift off — the 11 Oct from-scratch failures predate these guards.
- Decisions sampled during training (temperature/entropy floor in the recipe); inputs byte-identical to SFT (Ret: question + weak's retrieved passages, never the pool; QA1: question + selected passages + weak answer).
- **Plan C contingency (only if the kill criterion fires):** counterfactual end-outcome labels — one all-weak episode plus one stage-flipped episode per stage (S+1 live episodes per task) — minting SFT-style labels from end outcomes.

## 10. MedAgentBench transfer rules (registered now, executed later)
1. **Pinned environment window:** anchors, θ sweep, and held-out test run inside one recorded software environment (GPU driver, torch, transformers logged at phase start; no updates until the held-out test is scored). Justification: greedy generation is exactly repeatable within one environment (60/60, 20 Oct) but floating-point kernels are known to vary across driver/library versions; pinning removes that risk before it can contaminate anchor-vs-sweep comparisons.
2. **Determinism spot-check at phase start:** ~5 calibration tasks run twice; byte-identical outputs required before the ~1,500-episode budget is spent.
3. Sweep design as registered 20 Oct: anchors first (always-weak, always-strong, live, 150 calib tasks); sparse grid of 8 candidate θ values centered on HotpotQA's operating region; one live pass per candidate; Rule B with the ±1 SE tie definition (≈ ±4 pts at n = 150); one global θ (risk-aware extension: one θ per action-risk class, later).
4. Calibration/test split 150/150 (15/15 per category) per team standard; boundary frozen once testing starts; GRPO trains on calib (internal ~120/30 fold for early stopping permitted).
5. **Transfer baseline (decided 21 Oct):** the applicable HotpotQA reference for MedAgentBench is **GRPO both-routers** — held-out test 76.23 @ 80.5% (n=1,481), 78.10 @ 79.0% (n=105), 75.80 @ 84.3% (size-matched 500) — because the cheaper mixed pair (label-trained retrieval + GRPO answering, 75.62 @ 70.8%) requires label-trained adapters that cannot exist on MedAgentBench. Deck reporting follows this: the result table's GRPO column quotes both-routers; the mixed pair stays on the frontier and thresholds slides. Expectation to register: SS-level accuracy at a cost nearer the both-routers result than the mixed pair's.
6. **Risk-conditioned reward constants (PROPOSED 21 Oct — one function, two parameter sets selected by the instance's risk class; registered only when the MedAgentBench pre-registration fixes the constants).** Low/medium risk = the validated HotpotQA setting (λ = 0.10, risk term dormant). High risk (record-mutating actions) = optionally asymmetric Q (a failed high-risk action scores below 0), smaller λ, risk term −λt·Σ max(0, τ − cᵢ) **active with Goodhart guards** (per-class τ; router calibration measured before/after; rolled back if calibration degrades — cᵢ is the router's own confidence, a gameable signal unless guarded), and stricter per-class runtime θ. Must be fixed before training: (a) the risk taxonomy, decidable from the instance before execution (first cut: read-only queries = low/medium, mutating POSTs = high); (b) per-class constants with written rationale; (c) per-class reporting with the noise rule. **Sequencing:** the first campaign runs the validated recipe + per-class θ only; the high-risk column activates as a conditional second step if per-class thresholds prove insufficient.

## §14 — Ladder interpretation adopted (26 Oct 2026, Jonathan's formulation)
- The router decides better because it KNOWS ITS OBJECTIVE, not because it is given our insights. The reward exists in the training code but reaches the router only as a slow statistical signal; stating it in the prompt gives the goal in language from the first decision (and at inference, the prompt is the only place that knowledge can live). The insights are conclusions the reward teaches on its own; stating them is redundant and measurably slightly harmful.
