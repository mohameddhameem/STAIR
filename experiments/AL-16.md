The MedAgentBench counterpart of AL-04 (proposed 2 Nov). Same recipe as on HotpotQA - every rung fine-tuned, checkpoints
on validation loss, a learnt switching rulebook, each fine-tuned rung alone as a fixed reference - adapted to an agent
benchmark: one model per attempt instead of a retriever and an answerer, and switching means restarting the task on a
larger model.
